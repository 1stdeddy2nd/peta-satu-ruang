# MC-032 — Build-in-public: LinkedIn progress posts

- **Status:** BACKLOG
- **Board:** backlog
- **Type:** marketing
- **Milestone:** post-deploy

## Why parked
Nothing is deployed yet. A post about software nobody can open collects "can I
try it?" replies we cannot answer, and burns the launch moment early.

Starts once the app is live (MC-033). From then on it is a per-sprint habit —
see `rules/00-workflow.md`.

## Cadence once live
One post per sprint, written after the sprint's work is confirmed. Continuity
matters more than polish; a steady rhythm builds the audience, a perfect
one-off does not.

## First post angle
"QGIS and ArcGIS both need an expert. Canva didn't make Photoshop simpler — it
made the outcome reachable without one. I'm trying that for maps."

Then what it actually does: upload a shapefile, pick a template, export a
print-ready sheet at an exact scale.

## Per-post checklist
- [ ] Lead with the user-visible change, not the technology
- [ ] Screenshot or short recording of the flow
- [ ] Honest about the current state — no implying more than ships
- [ ] Reference the previous post so the series reads as progress
- [ ] A specific question that informs the next sprint
      (e.g. "which analysis takes you the most steps today?" → feeds MC-029)
- [ ] Indonesian and English framing — beachhead audience first

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Posted, link recorded on the sprint's ticket.
- [ ] Comment feedback triaged into tickets, not built on the spot.
