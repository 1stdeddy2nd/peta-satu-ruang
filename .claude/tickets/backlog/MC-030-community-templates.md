# MC-030 — Community template sharing

- **Status:** BACKLOG
- **Board:** backlog
- **Type:** feature
- **Milestone:** post-v1

## Why parked
Needs accounts and persistence (MC-015) underneath it. Built-in templates cover
v1.

## Why it matters
The same argument as plugins, applied to layouts. Regional and institutional
map conventions vary; we should not be the ones encoding all of them. Built-in
templates are seeds to prove the shape.

## Functionality
- Publish a layout as a reusable template.
- Browse, preview and apply templates published by others.
- Templates carry regions and elements, not the publisher's data.
- Attribution, and some way to surface quality (usage count, curation).

## Open questions
- Moderation of published content.
- Are templates forkable and editable after applying?

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A template published by one account is usable by another.
- [ ] Applying it never leaks the publisher's data.
