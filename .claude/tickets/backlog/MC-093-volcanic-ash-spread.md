# MC-093 — Volcanic ash spread on the map

- **Status:** TODO — parked
- **Board:** backlog
- **Type:** feature
- **Milestone:** MVP v1

## Why parked

Built in MC-084 from GDACS's VAAC ash extents, removed in MC-092 at the
product owner's request: on the redesigned event map the grey polygons
competed with the markers above them, and a reader could not act on them.

Worth returning to, because ash is what actually closes airports and reaches
people hundreds of kilometres from a volcano — but it needs its own answer to
"what should I do about this", and a drawing that does not fight the events.
Ideas: only for the volcano whose popup is open, or a plain sentence
("abu ke arah tenggara sampai ~120 km") with no polygon at all.

Everything needed to rebuild it — source, licence, endpoints, matching rules
— is in MC-092 and the commit it names.
