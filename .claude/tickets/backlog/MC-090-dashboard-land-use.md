## Why it's parked
"Lahan" was a dock row from MC-078 marked CONTOH — a placeholder with no data
behind it, removed by MC-087 along with Banjir and Penduduk. It doesn't fit
the dashboard's current theme (real-time hazard awareness) at all; land use is
static, slow-changing reference data, closer to an analysis layer than
something a non-GIS person checks "is something happening right now."
